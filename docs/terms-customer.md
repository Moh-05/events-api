# Haflati — Terms for Customers

By creating an account, you agree to the following.

**Haflati is a marketplace, not the vendor.** Your booking is an agreement
between you and the vendor. We handle payment and provide refund and dispute
support.

**Booking and payment.** For services (photographers, makeup, DJs, halls) you
pay a **20% deposit** through the app and pay the remaining 80% directly to the
vendor. For products (flowers, gifts, dresses, cakes) you pay the **full price
up front**. Payment is through ShamCash, in Syrian Pounds.

**Paying does not confirm your booking** — the vendor must still approve it. If
they decline, you receive a full refund.

**Refunds.**

| Cancelled | You receive |
|---|---|
| Before the vendor approves | 100% |
| Within 24h of approval | 100% |
| Within 72h | 50% |
| After 72h | 0% |

If the vendor cancels, you receive 100% regardless of timing. Refunds are
processed manually and are not instant.

**Reviews and chat.** You may review a vendor once your booking is approved,
and message them once they approve a paid booking. Never arrange payment
outside the app — doing so removes every protection in these terms.

**Support.** Open a ticket any time. If a vendor fails to appear or delivers
something substantially different, we can cancel and refund you in full.

**Your data.** We store your name, phone number, and location (if permitted) to
show nearby vendors. Vendors see only what they need to fulfil your booking.

**Liability.** Haflati is not responsible for a vendor's service quality or for
failures by ShamCash or your bank. These terms may change; continued use means
acceptance.
