# Haflati — Terms for Store Owners
*(Flowers, Gifts, Dresses, Accessories, Candles, Cakes)*

By creating an account, you agree to the following.

**Account review.** Your store is not visible to customers until we approve it.
Once you have completed it, we will review your account within 48 hours. If we
approve it, customers can find and order from you. If we do not, we will send
you the reason; correct it and we will review your account again.

**How you are paid.** The customer pays the **full order total up front** —
there is no deposit. **Haflati takes 15%, calculated on the original order
total.**
*Example: an order of 100,000 SYP → Haflati takes 15,000 → **you receive
85,000**.*

**Discounts.** If you place a product on offer, **the discount is deducted
entirely from your share** — our 15% is always calculated on the original
price. An offer lasts up to one month, after which you must wait one week
before discounting the same product again.

**Orders and stock.** You see an order only after it is paid, then approve or
decline it. **Stock is deducted when you approve**, not when the customer
orders; if an item has run out by then, approval fails. Products hide
automatically at zero stock, and you may hide any product manually. Prices lock
at the moment of ordering, so later price changes do not affect existing
orders. You must deliver every order you approve, correctly and on time.

**Your earnings.** Funds are held until the order is **completed**, then become
withdrawable; orders complete automatically one day after the delivery date.
**You must add your ShamCash account before withdrawing.** A withdrawal
transfers your full balance and is sent manually, normally within 24 hours.

**Cancellations.** If the customer cancels before you approve, they receive
100% and you receive nothing. After approval: within 24h they receive 100%;
within 72h, 50%; after 72h, nothing and you keep your share. Stock is returned
to you. **If you request cancellation of an order you already approved, you
bear the full cost** — the customer is refunded 100% and our commission is
charged to you, which may leave your balance negative until settled.

**Suspension.** Haflati may suspend or ban your account for fraud, failure to
deliver, or abuse, and may require you to fulfil existing orders first. Money
genuinely owed for completed orders is not forfeited.

**Liability.** All amounts are in Syrian Pounds. Payments run through ShamCash,
and Haflati is not responsible for their failures or delays. **The sale itself
is a contract between you and the customer**, and we are not liable for product
quality or delivery. These terms may change; continued use means acceptance.
