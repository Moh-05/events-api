# Haflati — Terms for Service Providers
*(Photographers, Makeup Artists, DJs, Wedding Halls)*

By creating an account, you agree to the following.

**Account review.** Your profile is not visible to customers until we approve
it. Once you have completed it, we will review your account within 48 hours. If
we approve it, customers can find and book you. If we do not, we will send you
the reason; correct it and we will review your account again.

**How you are paid.** The customer pays a **20% deposit** through the app; you
collect the remaining 80% from them directly. **Haflati takes 15%, calculated
on the original package price.**
*Example: a 100,000 SYP package → the customer pays a 20,000 deposit → Haflati
takes 3,000 → you receive 17,000, then collect 80,000 from the customer.*

**Discounts.** If you place a package on offer, **the discount is deducted
entirely from your share** — our 15% is always calculated on the original
price. An offer lasts up to one month, after which you must wait one week
before discounting the same item again.

**Bookings.** You see a booking only after the deposit is paid, then approve or
decline it. You must honour every booking you approve, on the agreed date. Only
one booking per date is possible.

**Your earnings.** Funds are held until the booking is **completed**, then
become withdrawable; bookings complete automatically one day after the event
date. **You must add your ShamCash account before withdrawing.** A withdrawal
transfers your full balance and is sent manually, normally within 24 hours.

**Cancellations.** If the customer cancels before you approve, they receive
100% and you receive nothing. After approval: within 24h they receive 100%;
within 72h, 50%; after 72h, nothing and you keep your share. **If you request
cancellation of a booking you already approved, you bear the full cost** — the
customer is refunded 100% and our commission is charged to you, which may leave
your balance negative until settled.

**Suspension.** Haflati may suspend or ban your account for fraud, no-shows, or
abuse, and may require you to complete existing bookings first. Money genuinely
owed for completed work is not forfeited.

**Liability.** All amounts are in Syrian Pounds. Payments run through ShamCash,
and Haflati is not responsible for their failures or delays. **The service
itself is a contract between you and the customer**, and we are not liable for
its quality. These terms may change; continued use means acceptance.
