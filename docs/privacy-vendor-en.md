# Haflati — Privacy Policy
### For Vendors

*Last updated: 19 August 2026*

This Policy explains what data we collect, why, and with whom it is shared.

---

**1. What we collect.**

- **From you:** mobile number, name, date of birth, business name, vendor
  category, business location (coordinates and address), profile and cover
  images, biography, and preferred language.
- **Your listings:** products and packages with their descriptions, prices,
  stock quantities, images, and portfolio items.
- **Financial data:** your ShamCash account identifier for payouts, your wallet
  ledger, commission and payout records, and withdrawal requests.
- **From your use of the platform:** bookings you receive, your acceptance or
  refusal of them, response times, blocked dates, reviews you receive, messages
  with customers and with support, and a device identifier for notifications.

**We never collect your ShamCash password or banking credentials.** We store
only the account identifier you enter, in order to remit your payouts.

**2. Why we collect it.** To review and approve your account, display your
business to customers, operate bookings and payments, calculate commission and
your balance, remit withdrawals, enable communication with customers, resolve
disputes, and prevent fraud.

**3. What is public.** Your **business name, category, location, biography,
images, listings, rating, and reviews are visible to all customers.** Your
average response time is also displayed. Your **mobile number is disclosed only
to a customer whose order requires delivery**, and your date of birth and
ShamCash account are never shown to customers.

**4. What administrators see.** Our administrators may access your full
account, your listings, your wallet ledger, your ShamCash account identifier,
and your messages, in order to review your application, remit your payouts,
investigate reports, and resolve disputes. Sensitive administrative actions are
recorded in an audit log.

**5. Third parties.** **ShamCash** processes payments and payouts. **Firebase
(Google)** delivers notifications and receives your device identifier.
**Supabase** hosts your uploaded images. **UltraMsg** delivers your
verification code via WhatsApp. **We do not sell your data or disclose it for
advertising.**

**6. Messages.** Messages with customers and with support are stored on our
servers. **Our support team may review them where a dispute arises**, in order
to establish what occurred.

**7. Retention.** Your data is retained while your account is active. Booking,
payment, commission, and withdrawal records are retained thereafter for
accounting purposes and for resolving any later dispute. **Suspension of an
account does not erase its records**, and sums genuinely due to you are not
forfeited.

**8. Your rights.** You may review and amend your profile, listings, and
ShamCash account within the application at any time, and disable notifications
through your device settings. To close your account, contact us through
support; outstanding bookings must be settled first, and financial records will
be retained.

**9. Security.** Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is
restricted to authorised personnel. No system can be guaranteed entirely
secure, and you are responsible for the device and mobile number associated
with your account.

**10. Amendment and governing law.** This Policy may be amended; continued use
constitutes acceptance. It is governed by the laws of the Syrian Arab Republic.

---

**By selecting "I Agree", you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this
Privacy Policy.**
