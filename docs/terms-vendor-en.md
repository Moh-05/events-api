**1. Vendor categories.** The platform recognises **service providers**
(photographers, makeup artists, disc jockeys, wedding halls), who operate on an
appointment and deposit basis; and **store owners** (flowers, gifts, dresses,
accessories, candles, confectionery), who operate on an order and full-payment
basis. Your category follows the vendor type selected at registration.

**2. Account review.** Your profile is not visible to customers until we
approve it. Once you have completed it, we will review your account within 48
hours. If approved, customers may find and book you. If not, we will send you
the reason; upon correcting it, your account will be reviewed again. All
information you provide must be accurate.

**3. Commission — 15%, always calculated on the original price before any
discount.**

- **Service providers:** the customer pays a **20% deposit** through the
  platform, and **you collect the remaining 80% directly from them**.
  *Example: a 100,000 SYP package → the customer pays a 20,000 deposit →
  commission 3,000 → 17,000 is credited to you, and you then collect 80,000
  from the customer.*
- **Store owners:** the customer pays the **full order value in advance**.
  *Example: an order of 100,000 SYP → commission 15,000 → 85,000 is credited
  to you.*

All amounts are in **Syrian Pounds** and processed through **ShamCash**.

**4. Discounts.** Where you place an item on offer, **the discount is borne
entirely by you**, as our commission is always calculated on the original
price. An offer runs for a maximum of one month, and one week must elapse
before the same item may be discounted again.

**5. Bookings and stock.** A booking becomes visible to you only after payment,
whereupon you may accept or decline. You are bound to fulfil every booking you
accept, on the agreed date and to the agreed standard. Service providers may
hold only one booking per date and may block dates on which they are otherwise
engaged. Store owners are responsible for their stock quantities; **stock is
deducted upon your acceptance**, items are withdrawn from display at zero
stock, and prices are fixed at the moment of ordering.

**6. Earnings and withdrawal.** Sums due are **held in escrow** until the
booking is **completed** — the service rendered or the order delivered — or
cancelled. A booking completes automatically one day after the event or
delivery date, and may not be marked complete before it. **You must register
your ShamCash account before requesting a withdrawal.** A withdrawal transfers
your entire available balance and is remitted manually, ordinarily within 24
hours. We may refuse an irregular or disputed request, in which case the sum
returns to your balance and the reason is communicated to you.

**7. Cancellation.** If the customer cancels before your acceptance, they are
refunded in full and no sum is due to you. After acceptance, they are refunded
100% within 24 hours, 50% within 72 hours, and nothing thereafter, in which
case you retain your share; cancelled orders restore your stock. **Where you
request cancellation of a booking you already accepted, you bear the entire
cost**: the customer is refunded in full and our commission is charged to your
account, which may leave your balance negative until settled.
