# Haflati — Privacy Policy
### For Customers

*Last updated: 19 August 2026*

This Policy explains what data we collect, why, and with whom it is shared.

---

**1. What we collect.**

- **From you:** mobile number, name, date of birth, city and address, profile
  photograph if you upload one, and preferred language.
- **With your permission:** your location, used solely to show nearby vendors
  and sort results by distance. The platform works without it.
- **From your use of the platform:** your bookings, payment records (amount,
  ShamCash transaction reference, sender name, status), reviews, messages with
  vendors and support, saved items, and a device identifier for notifications.

**We never collect your ShamCash password, banking credentials, or card
details.** Payment is executed within ShamCash; we receive only confirmation.

**2. Why we collect it.** To operate your account, show you relevant vendors,
execute and verify bookings and payments, send notifications about your
bookings, enable communication with vendors, resolve disputes and refunds, and
prevent fraud.

**3. What vendors see.** A vendor you book with receives only what is necessary
to fulfil that booking: your name, the booking details, and — where delivery is
required — your address and mobile number. **Vendors never receive your date of
birth, your precise location, or anything about your other bookings.**

**4. Third parties.** **ShamCash** processes payments and refunds. **Firebase
(Google)** delivers notifications and receives your device identifier.
**Supabase** hosts uploaded images. **UltraMsg** delivers your verification
code via WhatsApp. **We do not sell your data or disclose it for advertising.**

**5. Messages.** Messages with vendors and support are stored on our servers.
**Our support team may review them where a dispute arises**, in order to
establish what occurred. Do not send sensitive information through the
messaging facility.

**6. Retention.** Your data is retained while your account is active. Booking,
payment, and refund records are retained thereafter for accounting purposes and
for resolving any later dispute.

**7. Your rights.** You may review and amend your profile in the application,
withdraw location permission or disable notifications through your device
settings, and delete any review you have submitted. To delete your account,
contact us through support; records required for accounting or disputes may be
retained.

**8. Security.** Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is
restricted to authorised personnel. No system can be guaranteed entirely
secure, and you are responsible for the device and mobile number associated
with your account.

**9. Children.** The platform is not directed at persons under eighteen years
of age.

**10. Amendment and governing law.** This Policy may be amended; continued use
constitutes acceptance. It is governed by the laws of the Syrian Arab Republic.

---

**By selecting "I Agree", you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this
Privacy Policy.**
